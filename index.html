01 / ABOUT

Computational approaches to biological questions.

I am a PhD candidate in Biology at St. John's University, working at the intersection of bioinformatics, comparative genomics, transcriptomics, and molecular evolution. My research includes amphibian host–pathogen interactions, antimicrobial peptide discovery, genome assembly and annotation, and evolutionary analysis of immune and ion-channel genes.

I also bring experience in molecular diagnostics and clinical genomics, including next-generation sequencing analysis, variant interpretation, and computational oncology. I am interested in postdoctoral opportunities spanning evolutionary genomics, infectious disease, functional genomics, and translational bioinformatics.
